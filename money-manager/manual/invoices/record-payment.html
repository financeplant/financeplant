Record a payment and settlements

Use Record Payment from the invoice list or the invoice screen. The payment form is titled Record Client Payment.

The payment form

Invoice #The invoice you are paying.
Total InvoicedThe Grand Total of the invoice.
Balance DueWhat is still owed before this payment.
Payment AmountThe amount received. Tap Full Due to fill the balance.
Payment DateThe date the money was received.
Payment MethodSee the list below.
Transaction ID / Cheque # / Reference UTRThe reference from the bank, cheque or payment app. Example: TXN-89201948 or CHQ-4401.
Payment Notes / MemoAnything useful, for example "Received via wire, cleared on bank statement".
Settlement / Payment ClassificationWhat this payment is for. See below.

The screen shows Remaining after payment as you type, so you can check the balance before saving.

Payment methods

  • Cash
  • Bank Wire / Transfer
  • Cheque / Bank Draft
  • Credit / Debit Card
  • UPI / Instant Transfer
  • PayPal / Online Gateway
  • Other Electronic Transfer

Classification (settlement type)

Advance DepositMoney received before the work starts.
50% Advance / 25% InstallmentPart payments as agreed.
Partial InstallmentAny part of the balance.
Full ClearanceThe balance is paid in full.
Reconciliation SettlementUsed when matching a payment to an invoice after the fact.
Cash ReceiptA cash payment.

Save the payment

Tap Confirm & Record Payment. The invoice status updates: Partially paid or Settled in Full.

Delete a payment

Open the payment in the ledger on the invoice screen and tap Delete payment entry, then confirm. This changes the balance.

Record a payment only when the money is actually received. Use the reference from the bank or payment app so you can match it later.

Example: an advance and a final payment

  1. Customer pays 30% as an advance. Record it as Payment Amount 270 on a 900 invoice, classification 30% advance, method Bank Wire.
  2. Balance Due now shows 630.
  3. Customer pays the rest on completion. Record 630, classification Full Clearance.
  4. Status changes to Settled in Full.

Payment methods and references

  • Use the bank reference in the Transaction ID field so you can match it to the statement.
  • For a cheque, enter the cheque number.
  • For UPI or card payments, enter the transaction reference from the app.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Recording a payment twice. Check the ledger first.
  • Entering the wrong date. The date affects the aging report.
  • Choosing the wrong classification. It shows in the ledger and in reports.
