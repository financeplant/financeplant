Expenses and income

The Expenses tab is the transaction ledger, titled Transactions & Expenses Ledger. Every expense and income entry is recorded here.

Add a transaction

  1. Open the Expenses tab and tap New Transaction.
  2. Choose Expense or Income.
  3. Enter the Amount and the Date.
  4. Choose a Category and a Payment Method.
  5. Enter a Name if you want, for example a shop, client or person. Add Description and Notes (optional) if needed.
  6. Choose the Transaction currency if it is not your default.
  7. Optional: attach a Receipt. Tap Take photo or choose an image. Each image must be under 5 MB.
  8. Tap Save Transaction.

Edit or delete

Open an entry and tap Edit Transaction, change what you need, then tap Save Changes.

Filter and find

  • The ledger shows the number of matching records and totals for them.
  • Filter by type, category, date or payment method to narrow the list.
  • The Category Spending Summary ranks categories from the largest spend to the smallest.

Export

Tap Export CSV to save transactions and cash flow data as a CSV file, for example for a spreadsheet or your accountant.

Expense masters

Tap Expense Masters to manage categories, payment methods and sort fields.

Keep categories consistent. Reports and the Category Spending Summary group entries by category name.

Choosing expense or income

  • Expense: money you paid out, such as rent, groceries, fuel or a subscription.
  • Income: money you received, such as salary, a client payment or a refund.

Field-by-field guide

AmountThe value. Use the decimal point for cents.
DateThe date the money moved. Use the actual date, not the date you typed the entry.
CategoryGroups entries for the Category Spending Summary. Keep names consistent.
Payment MethodHow the money moved, such as cash or bank transfer. Used for filters.
NameThe shop, client or person. Useful for finding entries later.
DescriptionA short note about the entry.
Notes (optional)Anything else you want to remember, such as an invoice reference.
Receipt (optional)A photo of the receipt. Each image must be under 5 MB.
Transaction currencyUse this if the entry is in a currency other than your default.

Examples

  • Groceries: Expense, 84.20, category Food, payment method Card, name Supermarket.
  • Client payment: Income, 1,500, category Sales, payment method Bank Transfer, name the client, notes with the transfer reference.

Filter and search

  • Use the filters to show only expenses, only income, or one category.
  • The ledger shows how many entries match the filter.
  • Clear the filters to return to the full ledger.

Expense Masters

Expense Masters is where you manage categories, payment methods and the fields used to sort the ledger. Rename or add a category here, then use it when you record entries.

Common mistakes

  • Recording a transfer between your own accounts as both income and expense. Record it once as a transfer or skip it.
  • Using different spellings for the same category, which splits the totals.
  • Forgetting to attach a receipt when you need proof for a claim.
