Money Manager by Finance Plant

Money Manager helps you keep track of your money on your Android phone: what you spend, what you earn, what others owe you, and what you owe.

Download Money Manager on Google Play Read more about the app

What you can do

  • Record expenses and income, with a full transaction ledger.
  • Split shared costs with a group and see who owes whom.
  • Track loans and repayments.
  • Create tax invoices, record payments and print or share PDFs.
  • Set reminders for bills and due dates.
  • View reports and customer statements, and export them.
  • Ask the AI Adviser about your own records.
  • Protect the app with your phone lock.

Features

  • Expenses: Record and track every expense, see the full transaction ledger, and export to CSV.
  • Income: Record income and see your net cash flow.
  • Group Expense Splitter: Split bills with friends, family or a group, and settle up.
  • Loans: Track loans you have given or taken, with repayments.
  • Invoices: Create tax invoices, record payments and share them.
  • Reminders: Never miss a bill, payment or due date.
  • Reports: See revenue, aging and statements, and export them.
  • AI Adviser: Ask questions about your own records and get clear answers.
  • App Lock: Protect the app with your phone’s fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password.

Getting started

Follow the step-by-step user guide. It covers setup, daily use and backups.

Your data and privacy

Your records are stored on your phone. Google Drive backup is optional, uses your own Google account, and is encrypted with a password you choose. Nearby phone-to-phone sync is optional and only runs when you start it. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.

Requirements

Money Manager runs on Android. Get it from Google Play.
