Money manager by Finance plant

A product of Finance Plant

One app for your personal money and your small business. Record what you spend and earn, share costs with a group, track loans, bill customers and remind yourself of every due date.

Download on Google Play Read the user manual

What the app does

  • Record expenses and income with a full transaction ledger and receipt photos.
  • Split shared costs with a group and settle up.
  • Track loans and repayments, with schedules and late charges.
  • Create tax invoices, record payments, print PDFs and send customer statements.
  • Set due-date reminders and copy polite payment messages.
  • View revenue, aging and category reports, and export them.
  • Ask the AI Adviser about your own records.
  • Protect the app with your phone lock, and back up with an encrypted file.
