Totals, terms and bank details

Please note: Money manager is updated from time to time. Features, screens, button names and steps may change, and this manual may not always match the latest version of the app. If something in the app is different from this page, follow what you see in the app, and contact financeplantapp@gmail.com if you need help.

Totals update as you type. Check them before you issue the invoice.

Totals

Total excl. Tax (VAT/GST)The sum of the lines and before-tax charges, before tax.
Tax (VAT/GST)The tax calculated from your tax codes.
Round offA small adjustment to round the total.
Grand TotalThe amount the customer owes, including tax and charges.
Amount in WordsThe Grand Total written in words, for example Euro Twenty Thousand Only.
Balance DueThe Grand Total minus any payments received.

Amount in words

The words follow the setting in the invoice layout. You can choose the scale (for example Lakh and Crore for India) under Invoice Layout.

Terms and remarks

  • Invoice Specifications & Terms: the conditions you want printed with the invoice.
  • Footer Terms & Remarks: printed at the bottom of the invoice.
  • Scope Notes / Reference: notes about the work or delivery.

Bank details

Wire Remittance & Bank Instructions are printed on the invoice so the customer knows where to pay. Enter bank, wire or UPI details here. This box is optional.

Check every bank detail before you issue the invoice. A wrong account number on a printed invoice is hard to correct.

How the total is built

  1. Add the value of every line.
  2. Add before-tax charges.
  3. Apply the global discount, if any.
  4. Calculate tax on the taxable value.
  5. Add after-tax charges.
  6. Apply round off, if chosen.
  7. The result is the Grand Total.

Example

Lines 750 and 250 give 1,000. A 10% global discount gives 900. Tax at 20% on 900 is 180. The Grand Total is 1,080.

Terms wording

Write the payment terms the customer has agreed in plain words, for example "Payment within 30 days of invoice date by bank transfer".

Bank details

  • Enter the account name, bank name, account number or IBAN, and any routing code.
  • Check the bank details twice. They are printed on every invoice.
  • Leave the box empty if you do not want bank details printed.
