Money Manager user guide

Step-by-step instructions for every main part of the app.

1. Install and open the app

Download Money Manager from Google Play and open it. Accept the terms when asked.

2. Set up the app

Follow the first-launch setup screens and accept the terms when asked.

3. Use the Dashboard

The Dashboard shows total income, total expenses, net cash flow and group balances. Tap Refresh to update it.

4. Record expenses and income

Open Expenses, tap New Transaction, enter the amount, date and category, then save.

5. Split a bill

Open Splitter, create a group, add the members and record who paid. Use Settle Up when balances are cleared.

6. Track a loan

Open Loans, add the loan, and record each repayment as it is paid.

7. Create an invoice

Open Invoices, create a new invoice, add the line items and tax, then save. Use View to print or share the PDF, and Record Payment when money is received.

8. Set reminders

Open Reminders and add a reminder with its date, so upcoming dates are listed in one place.

9. View reports

Open Reports to see revenue, aging and statements. Export any report you need.

10. Ask the AI Adviser

Open AI Adviser and type your question. Answers are worked out from your records on your phone. Sharing to another AI app happens only if you choose it and confirm.

11. Lock the app

Open Settings, then App Lock. Turn it on to use your phone unlock. Set how long the app can be in the background before it asks again. The default is 15 minutes.

12. Back up your data

In Settings, you can set up an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. Keep your backup password safe: it is needed to restore on another phone.

13. Delete your account or data

Use the deletion page: Account and data deletion.
