Your money records stay with you

We built Finance Plant so your financial details are never sent to our servers.

  • Stored on your phone. Your transactions, invoices, loans and savings live on your device.
  • Backups you control. Backups are encrypted with a password you set, and you choose where they go.
  • No usage tracking. The app does not send usage or error counts from your device.
  • Clear permissions. Contacts are used only when you choose to import one, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions people ask

Quick answers before you download.

Is Finance Plant free?

Yes, you can download and use it for free. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob.

Who can see my financial records?

Only you. Your records are stored on your phone and are not sent to our servers. Sign-in and support messages are the only information that reaches us.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Make an encrypted backup, save it where you like, and restore it on your new phone with your backup password.

Is Finance Plant a bank or a financial adviser?

No. It is a record-keeping and calculation tool. It is not a bank, lender, accountant, lawyer or financial adviser. Check important calculations and decisions against your own agreements and professional advice.

Is it available in other languages?

Yes. You can choose your language when you first open the app, and change it later in Settings.

How do I get help?

Email financeplantapp@gmail.com or visit the support page.
