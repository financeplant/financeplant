Loans

The Loans tab tracks money lent or borrowed, with interest, installments and repayments.

Add a loan

  1. Open Loans and tap add loan.
  2. Enter the principal, the Annual interest % and the Date given.
  3. Choose an Interest method: Equal payments (interest + principal), Equal principal + interest, Interest monthly, principal at the end, or Interest monthly, principal in parts.
  4. Choose the day count: Day-wise (360 days a year) or Day-wise (365 days a year).
  5. Set the Installments, Installment every period and the First installment date (optional).
  6. Optional: set Grace days, Extra interest % / month when late, Guarantor, Collateral value and attachments.
  7. Check the schedule. The screen shows it once you enter the principal, rate and dates. Tap Confirm schedule.

Record a repayment

Open the loan, choose Record payment, and enter the amount, date and payment method. You can change the principal or interest of a payment; later payments recalculate.

Late payments

  • A late payment can be accepted without charges. Waive the late charges when you record that payment.
  • Continue normal interest: the option to keep normal interest running for the late period.
  • Overdue status shows the days past due (DPD) and a delinquency bucket.

Close a loan

Use Close loan without further payment to close it, or Close the loan after this payment to waive the remaining interest. Closing today would cost shows the pre-closure amount.

Change dates and history

Change due date moves a future installment. Activity log lists every change, and Details & History shows the full record.

Loan schedules are calculated by the app from the terms you enter. Check the schedule and the agreement with the other party before you confirm.

Before you record a loan

  • Agree the amount, interest rate, method and dates with the other party.
  • Write the terms in the Agreement notes so both sides can check them later.
  • Decide whether there is a guarantor or collateral, and record them.

Interest methods in detail

Equal payments (interest + principal)Every installment has the same total amount. Early installments include more interest.
Equal principal + interestEach installment repays an equal part of the principal plus the interest on it.
Interest monthly, principal at the endOnly interest is paid each month. The full principal is repaid at the end.
Interest monthly, principal in partsInterest is paid monthly and principal can be repaid in parts, whenever the borrower chooses.

Day count

Day-wise (360 days a year) and Day-wise (365 days a year) decide how interest is calculated per day. Use the one in your agreement.

Late payments

  • Grace days: a period after the due date with no late charge.
  • Extra interest % / month when late: charged on overdue amounts.
  • Waive the late charges when you record a late payment that you accept without charges.

Closing a loan early

  • Closing today would cost shows the amount needed to close the loan now, including interest accrued and any pre-closure charge.
  • Close the loan after this payment closes it and waives the remaining interest.
  • Close loan without further payment closes the loan without a further payment. Check the Activity log afterwards to see how the remaining balance is recorded.

Example

A loan of 10,000 at 12% a year, repaid in 12 equal installments. The Loans screen shows the schedule before you confirm. If the borrower pays late, the late charge and the new balance appear in the Activity log.

The app calculates the schedule from the terms you enter. Check it against the agreement before you confirm.
