Finance Plant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Finance Plant helps you keep personal and small-business finance records. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your phone, what is sent to our servers or to third parties, and the choices you have.

Contact: financeplantapp@gmail.com

1. Your financial records stay on your phone

Your transactions, accounts, invoices, loans, reminders, group expenses and other financial records are stored on your device. We do not receive your financial records on our servers.

2. Backups and group sharing that you control

  • Backups: you create a backup when you choose to. You choose where the backup is saved, for example a folder on your phone, or a destination you select through Android's share sheet, such as Google Drive. Once a backup leaves the phone, its copy is under the control of the service you chose.
  • Group sharing (Splitter): you can share a group's records with another phone directly, or as an encrypted file you send yourself. Shared data is encrypted with a sharing token that you give to the other person.
  • Nearby sync: when you use Nearby sync, the two phones connect directly to each other using Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location services on Android.

3. Sign-in and account data

Where sign-in is enabled in a release, you sign in with Google or Apple. The app receives a sign-in token from that provider and sends it to our server, which checks it and creates a Finance Plant account ID for you. Our server stores:

  • your Finance Plant account ID;
  • your email address, as provided by the sign-in provider;
  • an install ID, a device label, the platform and the app version;
  • a session token that keeps you signed in.

Sign-in is used for account access and support. It is not used to read your financial records.

4. Support and help messages

If you send a support message through the app or by email, the message, your account email and the details you include are stored on our server so we can reply. We keep these for our records and may delete them at any time. Please do not include bank passwords, card numbers or one-time codes in messages.

5. Usage and error reports

The app may send anonymous usage and error reports to our servers. These help us find and fix problems and improve future updates. They do not include your financial records.

6. Permissions

The app asks your Android phone for permissions to provide features such as reminders, receipt photos, voice entry, Nearby sync, ads and checking for updates. Your phone shows each permission when it is needed, and you can allow or deny it in your phone's settings. The permissions the app uses may change as features are added, and your phone's permission screens always show the current ones.

7. AI advisor (optional)

The AI advisor works on your phone. If you choose to ask a wider question in an outside AI app (for example Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude), the app sends your question and a short summary, such as totals and category names, without notes or contact details, to the AI app you picked. That app then handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

8. Advertising

Finance Plant currently shows advertisements from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google may show you a consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can accept or decline using the options on that form, and you can manage ad settings through your Android device settings and your Google account.

We may add other advertising providers in future. When we do, we will update this policy to name them.

9. Where data is processed

Your financial records stay on your device. Sign-in, support and any usage reports are processed by our server. Google (for sign-in, advertising and, depending on your phone, speech recognition) and any AI app or backup destination you choose process data under their own policies, which may involve transfers outside your country.

10. How long we keep data

Financial records stay on your phone until you erase them or uninstall the app. Account data is kept while your account exists. Support messages are kept for our records and may be deleted at any time.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and similar laws, you may have rights to access, correct or erase the personal data we hold about you. To use these rights, email financeplantapp@gmail.com.

12. Deleting your data

You can erase the data on your phone at any time from the app's settings. Backups or shared files you saved elsewhere must be deleted from that location. To delete your account data from our servers, email financeplantapp@gmail.com.

13. Children

Finance Plant is for adults. Do not use the app if you are under 18, and do not give us information about a child. If you believe a child has given us data, contact financeplantapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

14. Security

Your financial records stay on your device. Backups and group sharing files are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and your backup and sharing passwords private.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
