Install and first launch

Please note: Money manager is updated from time to time. Features, screens, button names and steps may change, and this manual may not always match the latest version of the app. If something in the app is different from this page, follow what you see in the app, and contact financeplantapp@gmail.com if you need help.

This page explains how to install Money manager and what you see the first time you open it.

Install the app

  1. Open Google Play on your Android phone.
  2. Search for Money manager by Finance plant, or open the Play Store link from financeplant.com.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

First launch

  • The app asks you to read and accept the Terms of Use and privacy notice. You must accept to continue.
  • You then reach the setup screens. Enter the details you are asked for and continue.
  • After setup you land on the Dashboard.

The screen layout

  • Header: the app name at the top. The three-dot menu on the right opens every section.
  • Banner area: a small ad space under the header. Sometimes it is empty while it loads.
  • Tab row: Dashboard, Expenses, Splitter, Loans, Invoices, Reminders, Reports, AI Adviser and Settings. Swipe the row sideways to see all tabs.
If a banner covers the tab row, use the three-dot menu at the top right. It opens the same list of sections.

Before you start

  • Have your phone unlocked and Google Play available.
  • Decide which currency you use most. You can still record entries in other currencies.
  • Decide whether you want App Lock. You can change this at any time in Settings.

Common first-launch questions

I cannot continue past the termsYou must accept the Terms of Use and privacy notice to use the app.
The tabs look cut offSwipe the tab row sideways, or use the three-dot menu at the top right, which lists every section.
A banner covers part of the screenThe banner is an advertisement space. The three-dot menu still gives you every section.

What to do next

  1. Open the Dashboard and read the totals (all zero on a new install).
  2. Add one expense and one income entry to see how the totals change.
  3. Set up App Lock in Settings if you want the phone unlock.
  4. Make your first encrypted backup in Settings → Backup & Restore Vault.
