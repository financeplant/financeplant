Create an invoice

Please note: Money manager is updated from time to time. Features, screens, button names and steps may change, and this manual may not always match the latest version of the app. If something in the app is different from this page, follow what you see in the app, and contact financeplantapp@gmail.com if you need help.

Tap Create New Invoice on the Invoices tab. The screen is titled Create Commercial Invoice when you finish.

1. Choose the profile and parties

Invoice profileThe profile holds your invoice layout and business details. Change it here only for this invoice.
Bill From (Our Entity Master)Your business entity. Set it up under Business Profile.
Default Business ProfileThe profile used when you do not choose another.
Bill To (Customer Master)Choose a saved customer, or pick -- Choose from Customer Master Directory --.
Client Legal NameThe customer name printed on the invoice.
Billing AddressPrinted under the customer name.
Customer tax numberThe customer’s tax ID, if they have one.
Dispatch / Ship FromTick Dispatch / Ship From is identical to Billing Address to reuse the billing address, or enter a different one.

2. Enter the invoice details

Invoice NumberUnique number for this invoice. Keep numbers in order.
Document TypeThe heading printed on the invoice, for example Tax Invoice.
Issue DateThe date the invoice is issued.
Payment termsChoose Due on delivery, Due upon receipt, 15th of next month, End of month, or Cash on Delivery (COD). Advance options are 100% Advance, 50% Advance, 50% Advance, 50% on Completion, 25% Advance, 75% on Completion, and 30% Advance, 70% on Completion.
Due dateCalculated from the issue date and terms. You can change it.
Invoice CurrencyThe currency of the invoice. You can choose 1, 2 or 3 decimal places.
Customer PO / Reference #The customer’s purchase order or reference.
Description / Scope of DeliverableA short description of what the invoice is for.

3. Save

Tap Generate & Issue Invoice. The invoice is saved and you can view, print or share it straight away.

Before you issue an invoice, check the customer name, the amounts and the tax. After an invoice has payments, the rules in Delete rules and invoice status apply.

Before you create an invoice

  • Set up the Business Profile with your name, address and tax number.
  • Add the customer under Customer Masters, or type the details once.
  • Check the Tax Master has the tax codes you need.

Example invoice

Invoice NumberINV-2026-0042
Issue Date21 November 2026
Payment termsEnd of month
Bill ToAcme Trading Ltd
Line itemConsulting, 10 hours, 75 each = 750
TaxStandard rate, 20% = 150
Grand Total900
Balance Due900 until payment is recorded

Tips for a clean invoice

  • Use a clear description that the customer will understand.
  • Fill the Customer PO / Reference # if the customer needs it to pay.
  • Check the currency before saving. Changing it later can confuse totals.
  • Use the Dispatch / Ship From field only when the goods go to a different address.

Common mistakes

  • Creating a new invoice when you meant to edit an existing one. Search the list first.
  • Leaving the due date at the default when the customer has agreed another date.
  • Forgetting to tick the tax registration box when you must charge tax.
