Expenses

Record and track every expense, see the full transaction ledger, and export to CSV.

What it does

  • Add an expense with date, amount, category and notes.
  • Use expense masters to keep your categories tidy.
  • Attach receipts when you need them.
  • Export the ledger to a CSV file for your accountant or spreadsheet.

How to open it

Open Expenses from the tab row under the banner, then tap New Transaction.

Tip: the section tabs sit under the banner ad. If the banner covers them, use the three-dot menu at the top right to reach every section.
