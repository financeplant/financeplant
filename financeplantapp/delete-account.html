Finance Plant — Account and Data Deletion

Finance Plant users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data using the instructions below.

How to request deletion

Email financeplantapp@gmail.com with the subject Finance Plant Account and Data Deletion Request.

In your message, provide the email address associated with your Finance Plant account so we can verify the request. Do not send your password or other login credentials.

What happens next

We will verify the request and, once confirmed, delete the account and associated personal data that we control, subject to any data we are legally required to retain. If any data must be retained, it will be retained only as required by applicable law.

We will contact you if we need further information to verify your request.
