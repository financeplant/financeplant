View, print, edit and share

Open an invoice from the list with View / PDF. The invoice screen is titled Invoice with its number, customer and totals.

What the invoice screen shows

  • The invoice number, Issue Date and Payment Terms.
  • Billed To (Customer Account) and Shipped / Delivered To.
  • The line items, tax, charges, Grand Total, Total Realized Collections and Balance Due.
  • The Payment & Settlement Ledger with every payment recorded.

Print or save as PDF

  1. Tap Print A4 / PDF.
  2. Choose your printer, or choose Save as PDF.
  3. Save the file to your phone.

Share

Tap Share to send the invoice through another app, such as email or WhatsApp. The app only shares when you choose.

Edit

Tap Edit to change the invoice, then save.

Back

Use Back to invoices to return to the list.

Print and share use the invoice layout set for the profile. See Invoice layout.

Printing well

  • Use A4 for formal invoices.
  • Check the Amount in Words and totals on the printed copy.
  • Save as PDF if the customer prefers email.

Sharing

  • Tap Share, then choose the app, such as email, WhatsApp or a file app.
  • Send the PDF rather than a screenshot, because the PDF keeps the layout.

Editing a sent invoice

You can edit an invoice after it is issued. Keep a copy of the old version for your records if the change matters to the customer. Payments already recorded stay on the invoice.

If you change an invoice after the customer has paid, check the Balance Due again before you send the new version.
