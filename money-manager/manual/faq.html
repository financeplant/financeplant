Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

You can download Money manager from Google Play. The app shows ads, and some ads are optional rewarded ads.

Where is my data stored?

Your records are stored on your phone. You can make an encrypted backup to a folder or your Google Drive. Read the privacy policy for details.

Does it work on iPhone?

Money manager is for Android. Download it from Google Play.

Can I use it for my business?

Yes. Invoices, customer masters, tax codes and reports are for businesses that bill customers. Check tax rules for your country with your accountant.

Can I share a group with friends?

Yes. Use the group sync file or nearby sync from the Splitter. See Group expense splitter.

How do I delete my data?

Use Account and data deletion, or delete the app from your phone.

How do I contact support?

Email financeplantapp@gmail.com or visit Support.
